# Pizza Sales Data Analysis

## Objective:
Conducted an in-depth data analysis of pizza sales using Power BI to uncover trends, customer preferences, and sales performance. The goal was to provide actionable insights to enhance business decision-making and strategic planning.

## Key Features:

## Sales Performance Analysis:
- Identified total revenue of 817.86K, with an average order value of 38.31 and 49,574 pizzas sold.
- Determined that the Classic Pizza category contributed the most to total sales and orders.
- Large pizzas accounted for the highest percentage of sales.

## Time-Based Insights:
- Orders peaked on weekends, especially on Fridays and Saturdays.
- July and January recorded the highest number of orders, indicating seasonal trends.

## Customer Trends:
- Analyzed daily and monthly order trends to identify peak sales periods.
- Found that customers on average purchased 2.32 pizzas per order, suggesting bundle sales opportunities.

## Best and Worst Sellers:
- Determined that the Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue.
- Identified The Classic Deluxe Pizza as the most sold Pizza in quantity and orders.
- Highlighted The Brie Carre Pizza as the least-performing pizza in terms of revenue, quantity, and orders.

## Technical Approach:
- Power BI: Designed interactive dashboards to visualize key sales metrics, order trends, and customer behavior.
- Data Analysis: Examined revenue distribution, order patterns, and customer preferences.
- Business Insights: Derived actionable recommendations to optimize sales strategy and marketing efforts.

## Results:
Delivered insights that identified peak sales periods, top-performing pizza categories, and customer preferences. The analysis supported targeted marketing strategies, product promotions, and inventory management.

## Skills:
Power BI, data visualization, business analytics, dashboard design, and data storytelling.
